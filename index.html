Got Questions?

Everything you need to know about ProspectInsight and lead mining.

Do I need any coding knowledge to use ProspectInsight?
None at all! ProspectInsight is an intuitive, web-based tool. Simply paste website URLs or upload a list, select the parameters you want to extract, and click Launch.
What file formats are supported for exporting mined data?
You can export your leads in 5 formats with full-length text: Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) with auto-fit columns, standard CSV (.csv), printable PDF tables, structured JSON (.json), and Google Sheets tab-separated text (.txt).
How does the daily credit reset work?
Every user receives a daily credit allowance (e.g. 50 on Free, 1,000 on Pro). Used credits automatically reset back to 100% every single night at 1:00 AM.
How are new user registrations approved?
When you register a new account, your request is reviewed by our administrators in the Admin Request Manager. Once approved, you can immediately sign in with full access.
